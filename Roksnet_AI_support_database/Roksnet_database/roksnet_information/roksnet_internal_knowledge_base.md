# Roksnet Solutions OÜ

## Overview

Roksnet Solutions OÜ is an Estonian technology company that specializes in digital public sector solutions and data exchange services. Founded in 2016, Roksnet has played a significant role in promoting digital government infrastructure worldwide. Roksnet works with governments, corporations, and other organizations to streamline digital interoperability and facilitate secure, automatic data exchange between systems.

## Our Story & Milestones

Roksnet was founded by a team of former public sector professionals including contributors to Estonia’s digital government infrastructure (X-Road, ID-card, Smart-ID, etc.), led by Riho Oks.

- **2017**: Launch of the Web Portal, Certificate Authority, and Trust Services.
- **2017–2025**: Active support and contribution to national X-Road deployments and NIIS codebase.
- **2024**: Launch of Global X-Road Services on version 7.6.1.

## Global Impact & Statistics

- 15+ countries using X-Road via Roksnet
- Over 25 billion secure transactions
- 0 security incidents
- 99% uptime using distributed P2P infrastructure

## Roksnet Services for X-Road®

### Member Directory

The Roksnet Member Registry lists verified legal entities authorized for X-Road®. It contains:
- Legal name, code, and classification
- Server/Web Portal IDs
- Certificate status

**Why it matters**:
- Ensures only trusted entities join
- Enables routing & discovery
- Supports legal audit & compliance

**For developers**:
- Provides trust & routing infrastructure
- Supports automation and interoperability

---

### Trust Services

X-Road uses digital certificates for encryption, authentication, and traceability.

**Roksnet Trust Services**:
- Global certificate issuance
- Remote onboarding
- Legal recognition
- HSM-secured certificates

**Third-party CAs**:
- Supported if compliant with legal & crypto standards
- Must use certified HSMs
- Includes QTSPs (eIDAS compliant)

---

### Web Portal

A browser-based interface to use X-Road without hosting a Security Server.

**Features**:
- Certificate management
- Secure audit logging
- Structured data exchange
- Integration with Member Directory

**Who it's for**:
- Non-IT orgs
- Government entities
- Cost-sensitive users
- Teams piloting X-Road®
